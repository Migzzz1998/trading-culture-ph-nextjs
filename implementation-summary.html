Trading Culture PH: UI Optimization Implementation

Two high-impact changes executed to maximize trust, transparency, and mobile conversion for the Philippine retail trading demographic.

01 $100 Deposit Clarity Campaign

Core Problem Solved
High Trust Impact
Filipino beginners are frequently exposed to MLM and trading scams that ask for upfront "membership fees" or "course payments." The original copy didn't explicitly clarify that the $100 deposit is 100% the user's own trading capital, not a fee paid to Trading Culture PH — creating hesitation at the conversion point.
Changes Made
Added explicit disclaimers across three critical conversion touchpoints:
Before
"Click the 'Join Our Community' button at the top of this page. You'll be guided to register with KVB (our broker partner) and fund your account with at least $100 USD."
After
"This $100 is 100% your own trading capital (never a fee paid to us, and fully withdrawable at any time). Once funded, you'll get immediate access to our Telegram/Discord channels…"
lib/config.js → FAQ "How do I join?"
lib/config.js → FAQ "What's the minimum deposit?"
components/HowItWorks.jsx → Step 03 "Fund Your Account"
components/Hero.jsx → New disclaimer box below CTAs

02 Sticky Mobile Bottom Action Bar

Core Problem Solved
Mobile Conversion
Over 80% of Filipino visitors browse on mobile via Facebook and TikTok. After scrolling past the hero, CTAs disappear, requiring users to scroll back up or hunt for navigation links. This creates friction at the exact moment a user decides to join.
Implementation
Built a lightweight, scroll-triggered fixed bottom bar (mobile-only, hidden on desktop) that keeps both conversion paths visible at all times:
  • Left Button: Free Messenger Group (low-friction observation tier)
  • Right Button: Join Community (primary KVB registration modal)
components/MobileBottomBar.jsx (new component created)
app/page.js (imported and rendered at bottom)
Key Features
• Appears after user scrolls 400px (past hero) • Backdrop blur + 95% opacity for premium feel • Messenger icon + "Free Group" label for clarity • Auto-hides on desktop (md:hidden) • Opens CoachSelectionModal on primary CTA tap
